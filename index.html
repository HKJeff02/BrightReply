BrightReply

AI drafts on-brand replies to customer reviews and flags high-risk ones.

BrightReply is in development and is not currently available. This website is a public holding page. The full product is parked: there is no account to create, no review platform to connect, and nothing to purchase.

What it is for

When BrightReply is offered, it is meant to draft replies that match a business’s voice and to flag reviews that may need a person, such as a refund request or a serious complaint.

Those features are not available today. This site does not connect to Google, Yelp, Trustpilot, or app stores.
